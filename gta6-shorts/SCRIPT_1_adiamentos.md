# Short 1 — The game that moved its own finish line (GTA 6 delays)

Format: 1080x1920, ~60 s, English voice-over, no captions. Original characters only (no Rockstar names, logos or trailer footage).

## Facts (checked 2026-11-19, re-check before publishing)
1. Trailer 1 came out on 5 Dec 2023 with a 2025 window; Guinness counted 90,421,491 YouTube views in 24 h, a record for a game reveal. (Guinness via PC Gamer, TechCrunch, PushSquare)
2. 16 May 2024: Take-Two narrowed the window to "Fall 2025". (gta-zone.com timeline, Variety)
3. 2 May 2025: Rockstar delayed it to 26 May 2026.
4. 6 Nov 2025: delayed again, to 19 Nov 2026. (Variety, "GTA 6 Release Delayed to November 2026")
5. Take-Two reaffirmed 19 Nov 2026 in May and Aug 2026. No third delay announced as of search time.
6. Take-Two's CEO said the game is "about 18 months behind the original date" (single-source, mark as quote or drop).
Open check: confirm today's launch status on Rockstar's site before final cut. Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only.

## Voice-over (~225 words, ~61 s at 3.7 w/s)
Hook: Ninety million people watched one trailer in a single day. Then the game missed its date. Twice.

Setting: December 2023. A studio shows a trailer and promises "2025". The internet does what the internet does.

Action:
- May 2024: "Okay, fall 2025." Everyone circles it on a calendar.
- May 2025: "Actually, May 2026." Everyone erases the circle and draws a new one.
- November 2025: "Actually, November 2026." Everyone buys a bigger eraser.

Snap: The calendar page is now more eraser dust than paper.

Deadpan double line: Luckily, the date has been confirmed again and again. Unluckily, "again and again" is a little suspicious.

Explanation: Think of a marathon where the finish line keeps moving. The runner is not slower. The line is just further away, because the course was being built while everyone was running on it. A game this big is the same. Every new street, crowd and detail is more road to pave.

Consequence: And that is why a studio would rather move the line than hand you a road with holes in it.

Callback twist: The fans waited so long that the calendar finally gave up first.
